Why at home

A cut on your terms

I speak your language

Castellano, català, english, português and deutsch. If you've just moved to Barcelona and struggle to explain how you want your hair, that won't happen here.

Reduced mobility

If leaving the house is the hard part, I'll come to you. Older clients, recovery after surgery or reduced mobility: no transport to arrange, nobody to depend on.

No waiting

No turning up to find three people ahead of you. Your slot is your slot.

Great for kids

At home they're relaxed, surrounded by their own things, without the barbershop stress.

I clean up after

I arrive, I work, and I leave it spotless. You won't sweep a single hair.
